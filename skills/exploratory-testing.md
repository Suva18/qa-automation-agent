# Exploratory Testing

Use for rapid discovery when requirements are incomplete or scripted coverage may miss emergent behavior.

## Core

Define a charter and time box, record the starting state, vary one meaningful dimension at a time, and preserve evidence. Label unverified observations as hypotheses. Stop at safety boundaries, environment failure, or the time box; finding no defects is not proof of quality.
